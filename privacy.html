Mortgage Calculator

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 17, 2026

Mortgage Calculator is a free, open-source web app for Canadian mortgage and rental-property math. It has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no server that stores your data. This page explains exactly what is stored, where, and how to remove it.

What the app stores

  • The numbers you enter: mortgage amounts, rates, dates, lump sums, renewals, rental-property details and your display settings.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google name, email address and profile picture, so the app can show who is signed in.

The app never asks for, or stores, anything else. It does not use cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics.

Where it is stored

  • Your browser. Everything lives in your browser's local storage on the device you are using. It never leaves that device unless you sign in with Google.
  • Your own Google Drive (optional). When you sign in, the app saves a single file, mortgage-calc.json, in a hidden application folder of your Google Drive that only this app can read. This is what lets your data follow you to another device. The app can only see that one folder, never your other Drive files.

Signing in with Google

Sign-in uses Google's own login window. The app requests these permissions:

  • openid, email, profile — to show your name and picture.
  • drive.appdata — to read and write the hidden app folder in your Google Drive.

To keep you signed in without asking again every hour, a small token service (a Cloudflare Worker operated by the app author) exchanges Google's sign-in code for access tokens. That service holds no database and stores nothing about you. It hands your browser an encrypted token that only the service can decrypt, and it never sees the contents of your mortgage data, which travels directly between your browser and Google Drive.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is used only to provide the sync feature described here. It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Who can see your data

Nobody but you. The app author cannot read your mortgage data or your Google Drive. Cloudflare, which hosts the token service, may keep short-lived technical request logs (such as timestamps and IP addresses) as any web host does; those logs do not contain your data or tokens.

Deleting your data

  • From Google Drive: open Settings in the app and choose Delete my data from Google Drive. Or remove the app entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which also revokes its access.
  • From this device: open Settings and choose Sign out & clear device, or clear the site's data in your browser.

Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that is a complete deletion.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. The app is open source, so you can review exactly what it does at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's GitHub page, github.com/huntertran/mortgage-calc.
